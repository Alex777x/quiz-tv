# Quiz TV — Permanent Project Rules

1. **Strict Ban on Russia-Specific Questions & References**:
   - The target audience lives in **Poland** (speaking Russian and Polish).
   - **NEVER** include questions about Russia, Russian ruble/banknotes, Moscow/Russian cities, Russian TV channels/shows/serials, Russian pop music/actors/writers/proverbs, or USSR/Russian domestic history/politics.
   - Questions must be strictly **international, European, or Polish** (world science, world geography, world cinema & music, world history, nature, technology, video games, European/Polish culture).

2. **No Fake `funFact` Placeholders**:
   - **NEVER** populate `funFact` / `funFactPl` with `"Правильный ответ — [ответ]"` or `"Историко-научный факт: правильный ответ — [ответ]"`.
   - Every `funFact` must either be a **real, informative explanation/fact** (without repeating `"Правильный ответ — ..."`), or `""` (empty string) when a question does not have a separate explanation, in which case the UI (`RevealScreen.kt` and `app.js`) hides the commentary box completely.

3. **Clean Editorial UI (No Emojis or Duplicate Badges)**:
   - Do not use cartoon emojis or flags in buttons or headers.
   - Do not add duplicate subtitle tags (`RU`, `PL`, `16+`, `SOLO`, `OK`) inside buttons.
